Colour

One theme, "Day". Bright fills take dark labels: action-500 and celebration-gold-500 carry ink-900, never white. Every text pair here clears WCAG AA on the grounds its note names.

Surfaces

Aa
surface-100#eef3fbPage background on practice/question screens and the calm app shell.
Aa
surface-200#ffffffRaised cards, panels, and modals on surface-100.
Aa
surface-300#dbe7f8Selected/active fill for calm-mode chips, progress tracks, and the PIN keypad.

Ink

Aa
ink-900#16202cPrimary text on surface-100, surface-200, surface-300, celebration-gold-500, and every -100 tint below.
Aa
ink-600#51617aSecondary text, captions, helper copy, and disabled-control labels on surface-100, surface-200, and surface-300.
Aa
ink-inverse#ffffffText and icons on the darker saturated fills only: brand-blue-600, celebration-coral-600, celebration-teal-600, and the skill -600 fills. Never on action-500 or celebration-gold-500 — those take ink-900.

Lines & focus

Aa
line-200#6a87bdBorders, dividers, and input outlines on surface-100 and surface-200; also the edge and press shelf of ghost and disabled buttons, where action-700 would over-promise.
Aa
focus-ring#0f62d6Solid 2px focus outline on any interactive control, on every surface. Never the only cue removed on blur.

Action — the one CTA colour

Aa
action-500#ff8a15THE one CTA color: the face of every Go / Continue / Submit button. Never used for anything else, so it always means 'do this next.' Takes action-on labels, never white.
Aa
action-600#ef7a00Hover face of action-500 (pointer devices only; touch goes straight to the pressed state). Takes action-on labels.
Aa
action-700#b85c00The solid edge of an action button: its 2px border all round plus the press-depth shelf below it. This edge, not the face, is what makes the button read as a raised object against surface-100.
Aa
action-on#16202cLabel and icon color on action-500 and action-600 fills. Dark on a bright fill reads better than white in direct sunlight, which is where these phones are used.
