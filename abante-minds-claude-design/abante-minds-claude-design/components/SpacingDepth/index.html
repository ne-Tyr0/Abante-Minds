Spacing, radius & depth

How far a pressable control sits above the page, and how far it travels when pressed. These three values are what make a tap feel like it landed.

Travel equals shelf height exactly. Disabled is drawn already-pressed.

Radius

radius-sm10pxAnswer tiles, inputs, PIN keypad keys.
radius-md16pxCards and panels: skill cards, profile card, modals.
radius-pill999pxEvery button and chip — the one consistent pill shape for anything tappable that means 'go.'

Depth

press-depth4pxThe shelf under a button: its resting height above the page, and the exact distance it travels down on press before the shelf collapses to nothing.
press-depth-lg6pxThe same mechanic on a large target — skill cards and answer tiles. A bigger object earns a deeper throw.
edge-width2pxThe solid darker border around every pressable control, in that control's own -700 or -600 shade. It carries the control's boundary against the page, which a pale fill on a pale surface cannot.

Shadow

shadow-cardRaised, non-pressable panels on surface-100 in calm/practice mode — the profile card, modals. Anything pressable uses the press-depth shelf instead, not a blur.
shadow-celebrationA gold glow for a badge or streak counter sitting on a calm surface, where it needs to feel like it arrived from Reward mode. Not used inside a celebration field itself, where a gold glow on gold is invisible.
