Row 6 — Tablet and desktop
Two rules hold the spacing together across devices: the gutter steps 24 → 32 → 48, and the reading column never exceeds 720. Tap targets stay at 48. The bottom-anchored action is a thumb-reach rule, so it survives on tablet and becomes an inline action on desktop, where the bottom nav turns into a left rail.
